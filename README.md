# HappyHorse AI Video

**[HappyHorse AI Video](https://happyhorse-ai.video/)** is a free online AI video generator that turns text prompts and images into cinematic short videos.

## Features

- **Text to Video** — describe a scene, get a video
- **Image to Video** — animate any image with smooth motion
- **1080P output** — high quality, no watermark on paid plans
- **Natural characters** — realistic human faces and movements
- **Commercial use** — full rights on all generated videos
